// Capabilities

A full trading desk, built for one strategy.

Everything between a rating gap and a filled order — surveillance, pricing, alerting, and one-tap execution.

Live market surveillance

WebSocket feed across every ITF market on Kalshi. Every tick, every book update, streamed in real time.

UTR fair-value engine

Rating gaps mapped to win probability — Δ2.0 → 99%, Δ1.0 → 85–90%, Δ0.5 → 65% — repriced on every update.

Instant mispricing alerts

Push notifications the moment market price drifts past your EV threshold. Never watch a screen again.

Manual approval, always

You stay in control. Review the numbers, approve the trade, and only then does an order leave the desk.

One-tap volume capture

A single button sweeps all available contracts at or better than your EV price — before the book moves.

Full trade ledger

Every fill logged with entry, fair value, EV and settlement — your P&L and hit-rate, always current.

// Workflow

From rating gap to filled order in four moves.

01 · WATCH

Track every ITF market

The engine subscribes to all live Kalshi tennis markets and mirrors the order book in memory.

02 · PRICE

Compute fair value

UTR gaps are converted to win probability and compared against the live market price, tick by tick.

03 · ALERT

Get pinged on edge

When EV clears your threshold, a push alert lands on your phone with all the numbers you need.

04 · EXECUTE

Approve & sweep

One tap places the order and captures every contract available at your price. The ledger updates instantly.
